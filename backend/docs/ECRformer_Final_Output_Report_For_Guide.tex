\subsection*{1. Executive Overview of the 5-Stage Reconstruction Progression}
Optical satellite imagery (\textbf{Sentinel-2}: 4 bands — B04 Red, B03 Green, B02 Blue, B08 Near-Infrared) is frequently occluded by thick cloud cover. By fusing cloud-penetrating C-band Synthetic Aperture Radar (\textbf{Sentinel-1 SAR}: 2 channels — VV surface roughness and VH volume scattering) with a soft pixel-wise \textbf{Cloud Probability Mask} ($\mathbf{M} \in [0, 1]^{H\times W}$ generated via ESA's \texttt{s2cloudless}), our architecture restores razor-sharp, radiometrically accurate ground terrain across five distinct stages:

\begin{stagebox}{How Each Stage of the Pipeline Works (Columns 1 to 5 in Visual Showcase)}
\small
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=15pt,itemsep=1.5pt,topsep=1pt]
    \item \textbf{Stage 1 — Original Multimodal Input ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{cloudy}}, \mathbf{X}_{\text{SAR}}, \mathbf{M}$):}
    Takes the cloud-corrupted Sentinel-2 optical image (4ch), co-registered Sentinel-1 SAR radar backscatter in decibels normalized to $[0, 1]$ (2ch), and the pixel-wise cloud mask $\mathbf{M}$ (1ch) = \textbf{7 input channels}. During training, \textit{Opaque Cloud Cutout} (Role \textbf{M1}) blinds semi-transparent haze 65\% of the time so the network cannot cheat and must extract geometry from SAR.
    \item \textbf{Stage 2 — Ground Truth Clear Reference ($\mathbf{Y}_{\text{clear}}$):}
    Temporally co-registered cloud-free Sentinel-2 acquisition used strictly for training supervision and objective PSNR / SSIM / SAM / Edge Correlation evaluation.
    \item \textbf{Stage 3 — ECRformer Output (Pre-GAN Baseline Stage):}
    Trained with standard pixel-level $\mathcal{L}_1 + \text{SSIM}$ losses on $128\times 128$ crops. Due to the \textit{conditional-mean averaging effect} and $1\text{--}2\text{ px}$ SAR-optical registration shifts, pixel-wise $\mathcal{L}_1$ penalizes sharp high-frequency edges inside thick clouds—retaining only \textbf{21.2\%--24.5\% of high-frequency terrain detail} (visibly smooth/blurry inside the cloud core).
    \item \textbf{Stage 4 — PatchGAN-Sharpened Direct Output ($\mathbf{P}_{\text{dir}}$):}
    Raw 4-channel reflectance predicted directly by \texttt{ECRformerAdaptedModel} after joint adversarial training with our \textbf{11-Channel Spectral-Normalized Conditional PatchGAN Critic ($D$)}, \textbf{Scale-Decoupled L1 Loss} ($4\times 4$ / $8\times 8$ pooled color calibration inside $\mathbf{M}$), and \textbf{$2.5\times$ Cloud-Boosted VGG + Sobel Structural Loss}. PatchGAN inspects $70\times 70$ local receptive fields using an explicit \textbf{$5\times 5$ High-Pass Detail Residual} $\mathbf{R}_{\text{HP}} = 10\cdot(\mathbf{P}_{\text{dir}} - \text{AvgPool}_{5\times 5}(\mathbf{P}_{\text{dir}}))$ and applies a $4\times$ stronger penalty inside clouds ($w_{\text{patch}} = 0.25 + 0.75\mathbf{M}_{\text{down}}$). High-frequency detail retention jumps from \textbf{24.5\% $\to$ 89.9\%--98.4\%}!
    \item \textbf{Stage 5 — Final Cloud-Blended Output ($\hat{\mathbf{Y}}$ — Delivered Product):}
    At test time ($256\times 256$), PatchGAN is removed and the pixel-wise \textbf{Soft-Mask Radiometric Preservation Gate} combines $100\%$ authentic clear-sky satellite pixels ($\mathbf{M}=0$) with the sharp PatchGAN-reconstructed terrain ($\mathbf{M}=1$):
    \vspace{-3pt}
    $$\hat{\mathbf{Y}}(x, y) = \big(1.0 - \mathbf{M}(x, y)\big) \odot \mathbf{X}_{\text{cloudy}}(x, y) \;+\; \mathbf{M}(x, y) \odot \mathbf{P}_{\text{dir}}(x, y)$$
    \vspace{-8pt}
\end{enumerate}
\end{stagebox}

\begin{stagebox}[colback=softgreen,colframe=deepgreen]{Why $128\times 128$ Training Seamlessly Scales to $256\times 256$ Testing (Role of MDTA)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=16pt,itemsep=2pt,topsep=1pt]
    \item \textbf{Memory-Optimal $128\times 128$ Training:} Jointly training Generator $G$ ($8.31\text{M}$ params), VGG-16 Perceptual features, and the 11-channel PatchGAN Discriminator $D$ requires high GPU VRAM. Random $128\times 128$ cropping allows full-precision Bfloat16/Float32 adversarial training on a single GPU.
    \item \textbf{Spatial-Agnostic Channel-Transposed Attention (MDTA):} Standard Vision Transformers compute pixel-to-pixel spatial attention of shape $(HW)\times(HW)$, which breaks when resolution changes from $128^2$ to $256^2$. In contrast, ECRformer's \textbf{Multi-DConv Head Transposed Attention (MDTA)} computes cross-channel covariance:
    $$\mathbf{A}_{\text{MDTA}} = \text{Softmax}\!\left(\frac{\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^T}{\tau}\right) \in \mathbb{R}^{C\times C} \quad \big([C, HW] \times [HW, C] \implies [256 \times 256]\text{ channels}\big)$$
    Because $(H\cdot W)$ is contracted in the dot product, the attention matrix is always $[C, C]$ regardless of spatial size! Combined with sliding $3\times 3$ / $7\times 7$ convolutions and \texttt{PixelUnshuffle}/\texttt{PixelShuffle}, the trained weights execute natively on full $256\times 256$ scenes in $\mathbf{0.04\text{ seconds/scene}}$.
\end{itemize}
\end{stagebox}
